Using the map

Connect RuneLite

  1. Enable the plugin, then open its sidebar on the same computer as your browser.
  2. Click Open RuneRadar. Allow local-network access if your browser asks for it. The pairing link expires after one minute and only works once.
  3. Log into Old School RuneScape. Your marker and account panel appear when a supported location is available.

The current development build is awaiting Plugin Hub submission and review. A public listing is not available yet.

Browse and follow

Search, drag or zoom to explore without being pulled back to your player. Your marker continues updating. Click the navigation arrow at the lower right, or press Space outside a text field, to return to your location and follow again. A filled arrow means following is active. Click it again to pause.

The layers button at the upper right controls points of interest, labels, theme and the account panel. Search includes full raid names and CoX, ToB and ToA. Pins, drawn paths and measuring tools are local map tools; they never control your game.

If the connection stops

  • After refreshing or reopening the map, click Open RuneRadar again in RuneLite to get a new link.
  • Keep RuneLite running. Logout and game-loading screens clear the marker until a new valid position arrives.
  • Instanced areas are currently unsupported. The map hides your position there to avoid showing an incorrect location.
  • If the browser denies local-network access, check its permission for this site and retry. If the connection is still blocked, check that the browser supports local-network access.
  • If the plugin says the port is unavailable, choose a free port in its settings and open a new link. For multiple clients, use a different port and browser tab for each.

Optional clue assistance

Enable RuneLite's Clue Scroll plugin and Show clue assistance in RuneRadar, then read a clue in game. Supported instructions and map targets appear in an objective card. Selecting a target pauses player-follow and switches to the target's floor. Turning assistance off clears the card and markers.

Clue assistance starts off. Some clues have instructions without a supported map target. Equipment requirements, combat warnings and puzzle overlays remain in RuneLite. Hot/cold search candidates are labeled as possible areas rather than exact dig tiles.

Quest assistance

Quest step sharing is experimental. It requires a compatible development version of Quest Helper and does not work with the current Plugin Hub release. The map shows an unavailable message when support is missing.

Report a problem. Include your browser and what happened, but do not post pairing links.
